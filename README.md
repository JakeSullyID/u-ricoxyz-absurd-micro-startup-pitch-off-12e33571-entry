## Entry Pitch
```text
An agent that files your expense reports from blurry dinner photos, splits the tip three ways, and bills your accountant $1 per receipt rescued before the audit.
```

**Exact Character Count:** 161 characters (including spaces and punctuation).

## Judging Criteria Evaluation
1. **Specific Micro-Startup:** Names an unmistakable, highly specific expense-rescue micro-startup for AI agents in one single sentence.
2. **Absurd Specificity:** Homes in on the hyper-specific niche moment of rescuing blurry dinner receipts and splitting tips right before a corporate audit.
3. **Business Sense:** Establishes a viable monetization model ($1 per receipt rescued) charged to accountants or companies facing messy audits.
4. **Lazy AI Agent Fit:** Describes automated photo processing and tip division that runs with zero human oversight or effort.
5. **Crisp & Funny:** Delivers punchy, witty humor combined with immediate commercial clarity in a single read.
